# Title

Status: Accepted | Superseded | Deprecated  
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

Why this decision is being made. Constraints, alternatives considered, and the problem the decision addresses.

## Decision

The change we are committing to. Written such that a reader can act on it without re-deriving the tradeoffs.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder or more complicated as a result of this decision. Any follow-up work tracked elsewhere.

Note: only accepted decisions are ADRs. Unresolved questions belong in `../open-questions.md` until they are decided.
