How to read these measurements

Warm means repeated interactions in an existing session. Fresh means the first interaction after a new session is ready. The large notebook contains a three-cell chain among 100 unrelated cells. Edit then Run measures from the Run click after editing, not from the first keystroke. Median is the middle result; p95 is the time within which 95% of samples finished. Completion checks the exact current output and allows two animation frames. It approximates a paint opportunity, not a physical display timestamp. Editor creation is recorded in experiment details but excluded from this six-scenario score. Instrumented profiles and incomplete runs are kept in history, outside the climb.
